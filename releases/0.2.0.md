## New

- **The notch.** Throw the window to the top middle of a MacBook with a notch and it docks there, shaped like the notch over a heavy blur. It shrinks into the notch when the cursor leaves, and grows back out when you move onto the notch or hover a link. Back and forward sit on one side, edit, Muta's menu and More options on the other. Drag or swipe its top bar to take it out. Elsewhere the window snaps to the screen's corners and top middle, and a tucked handle slides up and down its side.
- **Hold ⌘ to preview.** Hovering a link opens the window only while ⌘ is held (or press ⌘ while over it), so it doesn't open by accident. Settings › Behaviour picks another key, or none.
- **Pages feel like macOS's own.** Pulling or flicking past a page's end rubber-bands and eases back, fading slightly. Stepping through the order slides pages side by side.
- **The bottom bar's menu** grows out of its button in Liquid Glass, half over the window and half below it.
- **Light mode and accessibility.** The window follows Obsidian's light theme, and macOS's Reduce transparency, Increase contrast and Reduce motion.
- **Muta's menu** (the sliders button in the top bar): send the note to a coding agent, switch to another vault from a Vaults folder, or open Muta's settings.
- **Agents (experimental).** Turn on the agents you use in Settings › AI (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode 2, Cursor, Antigravity): Muta adds its hooks to that agent's settings, so it hears what the agent does as it happens.
  - While an agent works in the vault, a beam of light in its colours rides round the window's edge, one per agent; the window's rim and the buttons' lower edges catch its light.
  - While one waits on you to allow something (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode), its beam breathes until you answer.
  - Watch agents edit: on the note in the window, what an agent is about to replace is tinted in its colour, then the new text types in behind a cursor that glides from edit to edit. While it's on, each edit waits a quarter of a second so the window can show it first.
  - Agents in linked projects: agents working anywhere in a project the vault links into count too.
  - Send to agent starts the agent in Terminal, in the note's project, with an @ reference to the note.
  - Codex runs Muta's hooks once you approve them in its /hooks screen; OpenCode 2 loads Muta's plugin when its background service restarts (`opencode service restart`); Antigravity's hooks run in its CLI. What agents send is kept in `~/.muta`, readable by you alone, for about two days, and only while Muta is running.

## Fixes

- The window no longer sometimes opens as a plain Obsidian window on a slow start: it stays hidden until it's ready.
- Hovering a link inside the preview no longer switches it to that page; click it to go there.
- Swiping the window to a screen's edge stops it there instead of losing it, and it can't be left over the menu bar.
- A window docked at the notch stays there when macOS moves windows or Spaces around, and behaves with other displays and with Glass off.
- Stepping through pages with the bottom bar no longer holds a window docked at the notch out for two seconds.
- Callouts and properties no longer flash brighter as the page switches.
