## New

- **Done and failed.** When an agent finishes its turn in the vault, the window's border glows green for a moment, or red if the turn failed (an API error, say). It takes over from any beams while it shows. With the window in the notch, a thin ring of the colour lights round the notch instead, and the window stays in. Send to agent glows green once Terminal has it (red if Terminal couldn't open), and switching vault from Muta's menu glows red if the vault couldn't open.

## Fixes

- Docked at the notch with Obsidian in light mode, the window always stays dark; it sometimes turned light.
- Docked at the notch, the bottom bar's buttons sit on a soft backing like the buttons beside the notch, so they read over a white window below.
- Picking a note low in the bottom bar's list while docked at the notch keeps the window out a moment to show it, instead of it going straight back into the notch.
- The light on the buttons beside the notch keeps step with an agent's beam after the window docks or leaves the notch.
