While a local agent works in the vault, a beam of light in its colours rides round the window's edge, one per agent.

> [!use] Using it
> Run Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Antigravity on notes in the vault. While it's working, a beam of light in the agent's colours (Claude Code's terracotta, Codex's blues, OpenCode's greens, Antigravity's Google colours) rides round the page's edge, inside the window's frame, fading out as soon as it finishes. Settings › AI turns the beams off and picks which agents to watch.

> [!how] How it works
> `main.js`'s `AgentWatch` checks the agents' own records every 1.5s: Claude Code's session files under `~/.claude/projects` (only the folders named after the vault, or folders in or around it), Codex's under `~/.codex/sessions` (today's and yesterday's), OpenCode's database (`~/.local/share/opencode/opencode.db`, through macOS's `sqlite3`, read-only; OpenCode 2's `session_v2` and `session_message` tables, or the older `session`, `message` and `part`), and Antigravity's (`~/.gemini/antigravity-cli`: `conversation_summaries.db`, and a database per conversation whose steps hold the files it writes). A session is working while its record says it's mid-turn: Claude Code's last entry is a prompt, a tool's result or a reply calling a tool (not one ending the turn, its stop hooks or an interruption), Codex's last turn event is `task_started` (not `task_complete`), OpenCode 2's session started a turn (`time_busy`) after it last went idle (`time_idle`), or older OpenCode's latest message is the prompt or a reply unfinished or calling tools, Antigravity's conversation is `CASCADE_RUN_STATUS_RUNNING` or still has background tasks running (`not_fully_idle`). A record untouched for 10 minutes counts as stopped, whatever it says. Its edits (Claude Code's Edit and Write, Codex's patches, OpenCode's edit and write, the `Path` of Antigravity's file writes) say which files. Antigravity's steps have no times, so an edit dates from when Muta first sees it. It's working in the vault when it runs from inside the vault or edited one of its notes in the last five minutes, including notes the vault links to from elsewhere (symlinks, as the test vault's `features/` is). The beam follows [Beam](https://libraries.dev/beam) (`styles.css`): the agent's colours fixed round the edge (`data-agent` on each beam), shown through an arc that turns every 2.6s, as a 1.5px stroke round the page panel with a white head, a haze of its colours fading into the page over 74px, and a soft bloom at its head. All of it is masked rather than blurred (no CSS filters), so several beams at once stay at full frame rate. The window's outer edge catches its light, like the rim of macOS's glass (`.muta-agent-rim`): a softer, wider band of the same colours round where the head is, and a fringe a few degrees ahead with its colours shifted, splitting the light as glass does. With several agents at work at once, each has its own beam (and rim), spread evenly round the edge and shortened to its share (`--muta-beam-offset`, `--muta-beam-span`), all turning together (each starts at the same point of the 2.6s turn); they slide apart or together over 0.7s as agents start and finish, up to four. Each fades in over 0.6s and out over 0.5s; with Reduce motion it holds still and comes and goes at once.
