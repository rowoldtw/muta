Drag or swipe a tucked handle up and down its side; pull it 40px away to bring the window out.

> [!use] Using it
> Drag or two-finger swipe a tucked handle to slide it along its side; it stays where you leave it. Click it, or pull it 40px away from the side, to bring the window out.

> [!how] How it works
> `pip-main.js` keeps the handle on screen while it slides and saves its height; the window comes out at the resting spot on that side nearest the handle.
