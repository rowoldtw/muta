The page's buttons, Muta's menu and a close button; drag or two-finger swipe here to move the window.

> [!use] Using it
> **Swipe to move** in settings turns the swipes off. Muta's menu (the sliders button, in the right corner after close) holds what the window does beyond Obsidian's page: sending the note to an agent ([[Send to agent]]), Vaults (a folder of Obsidian's vaults by name, this one ticked: pick another to switch to it in this window, as Obsidian's own switcher does, or Manage vaults…) and Muta's settings. Docked at the notch it has no close button: back and forward sit left of the notch, edit, Muta's menu and More options right of it ([[Morph into the notch]]).
